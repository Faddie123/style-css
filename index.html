About NHA

The National Highway Authority (NHA) is responsible for the planning, construction, operation and maintenance of Pakistan's national highways and motorways.

  • Developing a modern road infrastructure
  • Ensuring safe and efficient travel
  • Supporting economic growth and connectivity
